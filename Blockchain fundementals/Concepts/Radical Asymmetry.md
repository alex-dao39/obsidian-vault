#### Concept
 - [[Technical Terms#^radical-asymmetry|Definition]]
 -  Modern cryptography is a radical asymmetry tool - meaning it is exponentially harder to attack than use.
 - E.g, Bitcoin and Ethereum private keys are 256 bits, which is secured from any brute-force exhaustion agains any [[Technical Terms#^advesary|adversary]].
 ***
### Attacks agains radical asymmetry
- **Implementation Failures**
	- leveraging inconsistent between design and implementation.
	- exploiting side-effects of implementation.
	- covertly replacing the software or hardware to change the functionality.
 - **Breaking the primitive**
	 - breaking the expected guarantees of the primitive.
- **By pass the cryptographic primitive**
